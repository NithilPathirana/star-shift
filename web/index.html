About Star Shift

Every six months, NASA's SPHEREx photographs the whole sky. Star Shift lets anyone flip through those photos, catch what moved, and prove it's real.

About the data

Each image is a 150″ cutout of SPHEREx infrared data from IRSA, lined up north up, 6.15″ per pixel. Hatched squares are pixels the archive flagged as unusable. SPHEREx images each detector at a different wavelength, so brightness changes between images mix wavelength and time.

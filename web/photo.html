Stars: Hipparcos (ESA), new reduction (van Leeuwen 2007), the same catalogue as the sky map. Planets and Moon: astronomy-engine (MIT, Don Cross). Method: star-pattern matching with four-star "quads", the idea behind astrometry.net (Lang, Hogg, Mierle, Blanton & Roweis 2010, AJ 139, 1782). Infrared images: NASA SPHEREx via IRSA.
